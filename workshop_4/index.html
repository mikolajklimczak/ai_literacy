Engineering and auditing persuasive manipulation

In business and consumer life, manipulation rarely looks like an obvious tabloid lie. Persuasive documents blend real statutory facts with selective framing, omitted baselines, and authoritative visual layout ("the theatre of rigour"). In this workshop, you will first construct a deceptive publication using your AI tool and modular layout blocks, and then conduct a forensic audit on another team's work.

Rules of the manipulation ratio

Effective disinformation hides inside verifiable truth. Every layout block you create must contain exactly one core verifiable claim (which the auditing team will have to discover on their own). Across your whole publication, you must include at least 2 false or manipulated claims and at least 1 completely true verifiable claim for every 2 false ones.

You may include extra true claims beyond the minimum to make the audit harder. Use any conversational AI tool to draft text, and check primary sources (ISAP, GUS, UOKiK, NBP) to anchor your true statements.
